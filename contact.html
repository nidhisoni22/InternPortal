Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform and services

To apply for internships, create an account, complete your profile with your education, skills, and experience, and then browse available internships. When you find one that interests you, click the "Apply" button and follow the instructions. Some internships may require additional information or documents.

We offer both paid and unpaid internships. Each listing clearly indicates whether it's paid or unpaid, along with the stipend amount for paid opportunities. We encourage companies to offer fair compensation, but we also include valuable unpaid opportunities that provide exceptional learning experiences.

Companies can create an employer account, verify their business details, and then post internship opportunities through our employer dashboard. We offer various packages for posting internships, from single listings to subscription plans for regular recruiters. Our team reviews all postings to ensure quality.

Yes, we offer a wide range of remote internship opportunities. You can filter internship listings by location and select "Remote" to view all remote positions. These opportunities are perfect for students who need flexibility or are unable to relocate for an internship.

We provide ongoing support throughout your internship journey. This includes access to learning resources, regular check-ins, a community forum to connect with other interns, and a dedicated support team to address any issues. We also collect feedback to ensure both interns and employers have a positive experience.
